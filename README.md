A single funny line of at most 200 characters, written by a program that
prints it to /output/entry.txt — the one-line elevator pitch a city pigeon
gives investors for its startup.

> Investors, my startup is a credit bureau: 40,000 pigeons watch your windows, count your crumbs, and know exactly who you had over last night. We sell that data. Your score is one coo.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
